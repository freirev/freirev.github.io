About

I am a PhD student in the ROCC Team at the University of Colorado Boulder, where I am advised by Prof. Marco M. Nicotra. My research focuses on combining control barrier functions with elements from the reference governor literature.

I became interested in control theory during my masters at the University of Wisconsin–Madison with Prof. Xiangru Xu, where I worked on motion planning for quadcopters and autonomous vehicles leveraging the differential flatness property.
